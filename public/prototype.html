How we make(02)

Paper, ink, machines, hands — and plenty of attention to detail.

From the first digital proof to the physical press run, every piece is made slowly and pressed one impression at a time on restored vintage Heidelberg platen presses.

01 · Pure Pigment Inks

Oil-based custom pigments mixed by hand to match bespoke Pantone shades and natural earth tones.

02 · The Mechanical Bite

Calibrated metal relief plates biting deep into thick cotton rag, creating indelible sculptural depth.

03 · Hand-Fed Presswork

Every single card is hand-fed into 1950s Heidelberg platens restored bolt-by-bolt in Dimapur.

04 · Archival Finishing

Hand-torn deckled edges, mirror foil edge gilding, and organic wax seals cast from brass matrices.
